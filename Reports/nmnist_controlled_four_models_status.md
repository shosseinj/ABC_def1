# Controlled local four-model N-MNIST comparison

Status: IN PROGRESS. The published Table 1 and Table 2 values remain reference numbers and are not the controlled comparison.

The controlled experiment trains the local custom SNN plus the paper repository's ConvNet (`SimpleNet_v2`), Spiking ResNet18, and VGGSNN on the same official N-MNIST train/test partition. For each representation separately, all models use the same equal-event-count `T=10` cache, seed 42, stratified 55,000/5,000 train/validation split, Adam with the revised common learning rate 0.0003 and weight decay 0.0001, batch size 64, up to 15 epochs, and best validation accuracy/loss checkpoint selection. Binary and Integer are distinct training campaigns. All four architectures are trained fresh under the revised recipe. A 95% full-test clean-accuracy gate prevents undertrained models from entering the attack comparison.

The copied source files for `SimpleNet_v2`, `ResNet18`, and `VGGSNN` have identical Git blob hashes to `models/simplenet.py` (`4354e347f132ddba6f7634f4c85bd0156481160a`), `models/resnet.py` (`a9b6b99d49dc18ca10ea82eda7943636418f2390`), and `models/vgg.py` (`dd15feef4d3e590e9b7709ce18608f1cbc0501ad`) at official commit `f16738f80be94067223f4968c1c318a125b9a321`.

After training, a single deterministic manifest selects the first 1,000 official test examples correctly classified by all four models. Every model receives the same sample IDs under each budget. The official-source PIL-PGD adapter, frozen representation, budget definitions, iteration counts, and independent raw-event reconstruction auditor are shared. This is a **new controlled local comparison**, not a replication of the paper's unavailable N-MNIST checkpoints or unspecified training details. The official source's hard-coded B0 penalty of 5 is used consistently for all four models; this differs from the paper's general-text value of 10 and is disclosed.

Current stage: ConvNet Binary completed at 96.68% clean accuracy under the initial common learning rate 0.001. ResNet18 Binary collapsed under that rate (25.58% validation after epoch 1), so its training was stopped and excluded. Under a second common 0.0003 campaign, the Binary custom model completed at 98.42% official-test clean accuracy and ConvNet at 96.83%; however ResNet18 fell from 95.40% validation at epoch 2 to 50.28% at epoch 3. That campaign was stopped and cannot be used as a fair four-model result. A three-epoch, validation-only ResNet18 pilot at 0.0001 yielded 96.84%, 95.70%, and 96.98% validation accuracy without collapse. The common 0.0001 recipe is frozen. Under `Reports/results/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4/`, Binary custom, ConvNet, and ResNet18 completed at 97.99%, 96.90%, and 98.61% official-test clean accuracy. ResNet18's best checkpoint was epoch 6; its training subsequently collapsed and early stopping triggered at epoch 10, so later weights were excluded. Binary VGGSNN is training. Earlier campaign checkpoints are not mixed into this one. No controlled four-model ASR has been validated. The existing `readme_jafar.md` paper-reference tables remain published-reference context. Runner: `scripts/run_nmnist_controlled_four_models.ps1` (first pass with `-CriticalOnly`, then full budgets).
