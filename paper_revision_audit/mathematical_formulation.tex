\section{Mathematical formulation}
Let $E=\{(t_i,x_i,y_i,p_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ be an ordered N-MNIST event stream and $X\in\mathbb{N}_0^{T\times 2\times34\times34}$ its temporal-grid representation, with $T=10$ in the controlled experiment. Binary representation replaces each positive count by one; integer representation retains the cell count. The two experiments use different time binning and must be described separately.

For the compact SNN, $I_t^{(1)}=\operatorname{Pool}(\operatorname{BN}_1(\operatorname{Conv}_1(X_t)))$, $S_t^{(1)},U_t^{(1)}=\operatorname{LIF}(I_t^{(1)},U_{t-1}^{(1)})$, and $I_t^{(2)}=\operatorname{Pool}(\operatorname{BN}_2(\operatorname{Conv}_2(S_t^{(1)})))$. Here $\widetilde U_t=\lambda U_{t-1}+I_t$, $S_t=\mathbf1[\widetilde U_t\ge1]$, $U_t=\widetilde U_t-S_t$, $\lambda=0.5$, and the backward surrogate is $\partial S/\partial(\widetilde U-1)=(1+5|\widetilde U-1|)^{-2}$. The logits are $z(X)=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T W\operatorname{vec}(S_t^{(2)})+b$.

For QSNN-v3, a spatial Conv/LIF extractor returns $h\in\mathbb R^{32}$ from pooled spike-rate and final-membrane summaries. The wrapper first computes $r=\tanh(Ah+c)\in\mathbb R^8$; the quantum head applies a second learned affine angle projection and clamps it, $a=\pi\operatorname{clip}(Br+d,-1,1)$. Starting at $|0\rangle^{\otimes8}$, each of two blocks applies data $R_y(a_q)$, data-dependent $R_z(\gamma_{bq}a_q)$, trainable $R_y(\theta_{bq})R_z(\phi_{bq})$, and ring CNOTs. A trainable measurement-basis rotation precedes $p_k=|\langle k|\psi\rangle|^2$ for $k=0,\ldots,255$; logits are $Wp+b$. Gate order is defined exactly by `models/nmnist_hybrid_qsnn.py:246-279`; this paragraph is schematic, not an algebraic replacement for that order.

For native-timestamp attacks, the feasible set is $\mathcal F_\epsilon(E)=\{t': |t_i'-t_i|\le\epsilon,\ t_1'\le\cdots\le t_N',\ t_i'\in[t_1,t_N]\}$ with $\epsilon=\rho(t_N-t_1+1)$. The white-box objective is $\max_{t'\in\mathcal F_\epsilon}\operatorname{CE}(f(F(E,t')),y)$; PGD uses 20 signed steps and projection. TEMP-DRIFT-v2 searches the same timestamp feasible set without gradients. See `scripts/run_nmnist_common_attack_protocol_seed42.py:55-80` and `scripts/run_nmnist_attack_protocol_v3_auditable_seed42.py:238-253`.

For grid retiming, define one packet for each nonzero source cell $(s,\ell)$, with fixed amplitude $a_{s\ell}=X_{s\ell}>0$. Its destination $d_{s\ell}\in\{0,\ldots,T-1\}$ stays on line $\ell$; distinct packets on a line have distinct destinations. No packet is created, split, deleted or amplitude-modified. Let $\Delta_i=d_i-s_i$. Then $B_\infty=\max_i|\Delta_i|$, $B_1=\sum_i|\Delta_i|$, and $B_0=\sum_i\mathbf1[\Delta_i\ne0]$. Amplitude does not weight $B_1$ or $B_0$. PIL-PGD maximizes true-label cross entropy through a soft retiming surrogate while evaluating a strict discrete projection; the source contains capacity and budget penalties. See `attacks/pil_pgd.py:49-139,266-331` and `AGENTS.md`.
