# Missing information, contradictions, and publication risks

1. **Two incompatible N-MNIST threat models.** QSNN-v3 PGD/TEMP perturbs native event timestamps under epsilon; the paper-aligned and controlled four-model campaign retimes amplitude-bearing temporal-grid cells under `B_inf/B1/B0`. Their budgets, sample sets, and denominators cannot be put in a single attack-comparison row. Sources: `readme_jafar.md:209-250`, `attacks/pil_pgd.py:1-14`, `scripts/run_nmnist_controlled_attack.py:25-133`.
2. **README is chronologically mixed.** Its Table 1/2 custom rows use older training and different checkpoints; its QSNN section predates the controlled four-model campaign, and an early status note says attacks paused while other sections report completion. Treat the per-run artifact and date as authority, and identify historical rows explicitly. Sources: `readme_jafar.md:1-10,119-159,175-190`, `Reports/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4_comparison.md`.
3. **Single seed for attack robustness.** The controlled campaign has only seed 42 and two attack budgets per representation. The earlier five-seed QSNN study is clean validation, not a five-seed robustness experiment. Multi-seed intervals and claims about population robustness are NOT VERIFIED. Sources: `configs/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4.json`, `readme_jafar.md:175-190,282-286`.
4. **Architecture-specific attack strength.** The local custom SNN reaches near-100% ASR while three baselines show 0–1.9% at the same budgets. Common settings are fair as an experimental design, but stronger convergence, gradient diagnostics, parameter sweeps and independent attacks are needed before asserting relative intrinsic robustness. Source: `Reports/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4_comparison.md`.
5. **Paper/source B0 ambiguity.** The upstream code reportedly fixes a B0 penalty of 5, while paper general text specifies 10; local comparison uses the same code across models but is not a literal verification of that paper hyperparameter. Source: `Reports/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4_comparison.md:27`, `experiments/nmnist/official_pil_adapter_batched.py`.

Do not present 97.272% as test accuracy or place it beside official-test controlled rows without split labels. Source: `results/nmnist_hybrid_qsnn_seed42/nmnist_qsnn_v3_multiseed/summary.json`.
