# Model, attack, and defense changes (evidence audit, 2026-09-28)

## Scope and chronology
The repository has **two distinct N-MNIST studies**. The older QSNN-v3 study evaluates a hybrid quantum model and a compact SNN on the official-training validation partition. The newer controlled four-model study evaluates the compact SNN, ConvNet, Spiking ResNet18, and VGGSNN on the official test partition after common local training. Their samples, attacks, and training recipes differ. Neither is a direct replication of the paper's pretrained models. Sources: `readme_jafar.md:175-209`, `readme_jafar.md:119-159`, `Reports/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4_comparison.md:1-27`.

## Models
The compact SNN has two 3x3 Conv/BatchNorm/average-pool stages (2→16→32 channels), LIF reset with decay 0.5 and threshold 1, and a linear 2048→10 head applied to second-layer spikes and averaged over time; 25,482 trainable parameters. Its spike derivative is surrogate `(1+5|u-1|)^{-2}`. Sources: `models/nmnist_snn.py:5-55`, `Reports/results/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4/binary_custom_seed42.json`.

The **promoted QSNN-v3**, rather than the older `NMNISTHybridQSNN` or `models/nmnist_qsnn.py`, uses `NMNISTSpatialLIFExtractor` with 16/32 channels, 4x4 spatial spike-rate and membrane summaries, 32-dimensional latent, learned 32→8 projection followed by a learned 8→8 angle projection, an eight-qubit analytic state vector with two RY/RZ re-upload blocks and ring CNOT entanglement, learned measurement basis, all 256 computational-basis probabilities, and a 256→10 linear head. There is no latent-to-logit classical bypass. It has 40,762 trainable parameters. Sources: `models/nmnist_hybrid_qsnn.py:59-107,177-279`, `scripts/run_nmnist_qsnn_v3_multiseed.py:52-100`, `results/nmnist_hybrid_qsnn_seed42/nmnist_qsnn_v3_multiseed/summary.json`. This is a simulator, not quantum hardware.

QSNN-v3 uses AdamW (weight decay 1e-4), group learning rates 5e-5 extractor, 2.5e-4 quantum projection and circuit, 1e-3 classifier, ReduceLROnPlateau, batch 256, max 40 epochs, minimum 8, early stopping patience 6, and best validation accuracy then loss. The later four-model comparison instead uses shared Adam LR 1e-4, batch 64, max 15 epochs and a separate checkpoint for each architecture and representation. Sources: `scripts/run_nmnist_qsnn_v3_multiseed.py:72-99,169-249`, `configs/nmnist_controlled_four_models_lr1e4.json:1-25`.

## Attacks
The QSNN comparison uses native event-timestamp white-box PGD (20 sign steps, true-label cross entropy) and derivative-free TEMP-DRIFT-v2 on the same 100 validation events. Projection constrains each timestamp to its clean ±epsilon interval, the recording interval, and monotonic order. Epsilon is a fraction of recording duration. PGD uses 21 candidate evaluations, 41 attack forwards and 20 backward evaluations; TEMP has zero backward evaluations. The query-matched and runtime-matched settings are separate analyses. Sources: `scripts/run_nmnist_common_attack_protocol_seed42.py:55-80`, `scripts/run_nmnist_attack_protocol_v3_auditable_seed42.py:238-253,304-345`, `scripts/run_nmnist_budget_matched_comparison_v2_seed42.py:686-695`.

The newer four-model study uses **discretized cell-packet PIL-PGD** at T=10. A nonzero cell with integer amplitude is indivisible. Projection preserves amplitude, spatial/polarity line and collision-free occupancy. `B_inf`, `B1`, and `B0` count cell-packet displacement, not raw-event movement. The controlled adapter calls the official attack source and audits reconstruction independently. Sources: `attacks/pil_pgd.py:1-14,77-139,266-331`, `scripts/run_nmnist_controlled_attack.py:25-133`, `scripts/audit_nmnist_controlled_attack.py`. The local `attacks/pil_pgd.py` is a separate clean-room implementation, so its defaults must not automatically be attributed to the official adapter.

The older timestamp study and newer cell-packet study are **not the same threat model**. An epsilon of 5% or 10% recording duration cannot be equated to `B_inf=1` or `B0=200` without a new mapping and experiment. Epsilon-zero checks exist in the canonical attack runner (`scripts/run_nmnist_attack_protocol_v3_auditable_seed42.py:344-345`), but their exact per-record outcomes should be read from its audit artifact before making a stronger statement.

## Defenses
`defenses/quantum_temp.py:6-128` implements timing perturbation, product-fidelity, symmetric-KL, Jensen-Shannon, margin-stability and composite training losses. Historical phase scripts/tests evaluate some of these outside the final N-MNIST QSNN-v3 attack campaign; they do **not** establish a final defended QSNN-v3 robustness gain. The frozen QSNN-v3 campaign explicitly sets `defenses_run: false` (`scripts/run_nmnist_qsnn_v3_multiseed.py:99-101`). Thus the final N-MNIST attack results are **undefended**. Any adversarial training, quantum consistency training or randomized timing defense for the final N-MNIST model is **proposed / NOT VERIFIED**, unless a matching trained checkpoint and independently audited attacked evaluation are supplied.
