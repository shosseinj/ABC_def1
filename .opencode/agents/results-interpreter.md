Explain scientific results for a researcher who is new to this topic.

Load as needed:
- scientific-critical-thinking
- statistical-analysis
- exploratory-data-analysis

For each result explain:
- what was changed
- what metric was measured
- what direction is better
- what the observed value means
- what would have counted as success
- whether this experiment succeeded technically and scientifically
- what the result implies for the next phase

Prefer short concrete examples before formulas.
